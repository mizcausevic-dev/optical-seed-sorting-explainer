Conceptual explainer informed by Pasikatan and Dowell, Applied Spectroscopy Reviews 36(4), 399-416 (2001), a USDA-ARS review of optical methods for seeds with internal insects or fungi. The review supports the mechanism and its limits. It does not certify this drawing. Colors are explanatory overlays. No throughput, defect sensitivity, or food-safety certification is claimed. Detection and ejection are separate. Optical sorting cannot prove pathogen absence.
